EU / MULTI-COUNTRY PLANNING

EU EPR by Country: Plan Multi-Market Sales

EU-wide service coverage is a capability; country compliance is a set of specific arrangements. Use one planning worksheet while keeping each destination’s evidence and responsibilities distinct.

Choose a realistic market sequence

Separate countries already generating sales from those planned for later. Record the business reason, launch date and responsible owner for each. For US, UK and Chinese companies, use the same legal-entity details throughout the plan and flag any EU importer or distributor involved in the transaction.

Use one row per country and waste stream

For each row, record the assessed producer, packaging or product category, register or scheme, mandate status, quantities, evidence and reporting calendar. Do not combine household packaging, electrical equipment and batteries into a single unqualified “EPR complete” field. The Commission directs businesses to the applicable national packaging rules.

Primary source

Start the Belgian row with usable data

Identify the household and industrial/commercial packaging routes, prepare material and weight records, and reconcile Belgian quantities with orders. Keep the scheme contract, producer-register evidence and any mandate separately. Then add other EU destinations using the same record structure, with a fresh national assessment.

Review the plan when the business changes

Create review triggers for new countries, company changes, new brands, packaging redesigns and new product streams. Set out who checks rule changes and who supplies data. BelComply supports all 27 EU member states with Belgium as its core focus; the agreed work for each destination should be visible in the proposal.
