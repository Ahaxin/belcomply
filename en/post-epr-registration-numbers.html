PPWR / REGISTRATION & REPRESENTATION

EU EPR Numbers: Registration vs Membership

Treat “EPR number” as a request to identify a specific record, not as a universal identifier. First establish the country, waste stream, issuing body and legal company named on the evidence.

Four records that should not be interchanged

A producer-register confirmation records registration with the relevant register. A scheme membership record concerns the arrangement with that scheme. A mandate identifies an appointment and its scope. An application receipt shows submission, which should not be presented as approval. Store the original document and its date, not only a number copied into a spreadsheet.

Belgium: ask for the actual registration confirmation

Fost Plus’s June 2026 guidance says it handles producer registration collectively for members and provides the official number after authority confirmation. Membership and an expected registration therefore describe different stages. Check the status of your own file before supplying a number as completed registration evidence.

Germany: use the LUCID evidence for the right entity

ZSVR’s registration FAQ explains where the LUCID number appears: on the registration decision and dashboard. Verify the company and registration details against those records. Do not reuse another group company’s record merely because it operates the same brand or uses the same fulfilment account.

Build a marketplace evidence row

For each request, record the selling account, country, stream, requested field, issuer, named entity, number, source document and response status. If the platform rejects the evidence, preserve the exact message and check for a scope or entity mismatch before applying again. A VAT number, scheme invoice or representative contract should not be substituted without confirmation that it is the requested evidence.
