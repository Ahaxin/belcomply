eBay packaging EPR: registration numbers and Italy Pay on Behalf

By BelComply · Published 18 October 2026 · Updated and sources checked 21 November 2026

Do eBay sellers need an EPR registration number?

Business sellers who are packaging producers in an EU destination should check the national registration and eBay evidence requirements for that market. Since the general application of PPWR on 12 August 2026, eBay asks affected sellers for country-specific packaging registration details and a compliance declaration. Its Italy Pay on Behalf (PoB) option is separate: it may handle eligible reporting and contributions, not automatically register a seller or cover sales elsewhere.

Which markets and evidence should you check?

First identify the legal seller, destination country and whether your business is the packaging producer there. The assessment depends on the selling route, including direct sales to customers, local stock or imports. An overseas business may need an EPR authorised representative where applicable. Used products are not automatically outside PPWR; the packaging used for shipping can still matter. The US eBay Seller Center and UK eBay PPWR help describe the platform's packaging requirements; confirm the country procedure separately.

How to add an EPR packaging number on eBay

  1. On a desktop or tablet, navigate to Account Settings and Business Info.
  2. Open the EPR number management area and choose Add or Manage.
  3. Choose the destination and packaging waste stream, then enter your verified number.
  4. Review the compliance declaration; confirm it only if accurate, and save the entry.
  5. Repeat for each relevant national registration and retain the platform confirmation.

eBay says existing French and German packaging entries transfer to the new packaging fields automatically. Check whether the details are current and update any inaccurate entry. eBay also says listings may be hidden in a destination if a required packaging number is absent or invalid. Platform acceptance is not proof that all national reporting and payment obligations are complete.

Italy EPR: what is eBay Pay on Behalf?

eBay's Italy guidance offers eligible business sellers an optional arrangement to have the platform calculate contributions, report and make payments for supported Italian sales and enrolled categories. It is not an EU-wide registration or a blanket exemption. The PoB terms describe the packaging part of this service for cross-border sellers shipping into Italy; do not read that as a statement that Italian domestic packaging businesses have no underlying EPR obligations.

QuestionWhat the Italy PoB terms mean for sellers
Which categories?Potential supported categories include packaging, tyres, polyethylene, electronics and batteries. Scope may change; check what your account offers and the relevant number. Other categories on the Italy help page are not automatically PoB-covered.
Do I still need a number?The published eBay Italy joining process starts with adding the relevant EPR number. Verify the national record and category first.
Is PoB free?eBay currently waives the service charge during the launch period; future charges are possible with notice. Do not interpret this as a universal waiver of the underlying EPR obligations or contributions.
What data is used?Listing and transaction information, categories and product attributes are used for estimates and reporting. Seller information must be accurate; the platform does not independently verify eligibility or data.
Where does coverage stop?Only the eligible Italian transactions and enrolled categories are within the arrangement. Review other channels, countries and categories separately.
Can I withdraw?According to the terms, withdrawal takes effect at the end of the quarter. Amounts relating to sales made during participation can still be calculated.

How to decide whether to join Italy Pay on Behalf

  1. Determine if the seller and packaging activity fall within the applicable Italian obligations. The PoB terms mention possible micro-enterprise exceptions, but eBay does not decide whether an exception applies to you.
  2. Check the underlying Italian route. CONAI explains that under Italy's Article 178-quater, qualifying marketplace sellers may use simplified procedures through platform agreements with EPR systems; a seller not using the available platform service must address its applicable obligations separately. Review current national rules for your situation.
  3. In eBay's Italy EPR management area, add the applicable number. When the PoB offer is visible, review its terms and choose the eligible categories. Do not assume the option has been activated without evidence.
  4. Keep a record of participation and its start date, the selected categories, any opt-out, underlying registrations, and available contribution and reporting records.

What if I sell to Italy and Belgium from the US or UK?

For example, a UK business shipping packaged goods to Italian and Belgian customers may have different national responsibilities. If it enrolls in Italian packaging PoB for eligible eBay transactions, that does not resolve its Belgian registration or sales made on its own website. Keep a country-and-channel breakdown, review any independent appointment and check the scope of each registration. US sellers should follow the same country-by-country assessment rather than treating the account's marketplace country as the producer's destination. See EU EPR for US companies and EU EPR for UK sellers.

Your eBay evidence checklist

  • Legal seller identity, actual establishment, destination markets, exact account notice and category.
  • Original registration or scheme evidence, issuer, scope and reporting period; representative mandate when required.
  • Material weights and quantities, including shipment packaging, split by country and channel.
  • Evidence field, confirmation and any discrepancy; correct the underlying data before resubmitting.
  • For Italy PoB, enrollment scope and dates, transaction breakdown and available reporting/payment records; retain records for applicable audits.

Packaging is not WEEE, battery EPR or GPSR. Assess each independently. BelComply can help you scope packaging registration, reporting and applicable PPWR representation; GPSR is a separate service. Acceptance decisions and programme eligibility remain with the relevant platform and authority.
