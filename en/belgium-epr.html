Belgium packaging EPR registration & authorised representative

A clear packaging compliance route for US, UK and Chinese businesses selling into Belgium.

What do foreign sellers need to check?

Identify the responsible producer, packaging stream, sales route and applicable Belgian rules. Packaging scheme membership, producer registration and a PPWR EPR representative mandate are distinct steps; GPSR is a separate product-safety service.

Who needs an EPR authorised representative?

The answer depends on where the producer is established, how packaging reaches Belgian end users and the rules applying to that route. PPWR Article 45(3) addresses certain producers established in another EU member state. For third-country businesses, including US, UK and Chinese entities, the applicable national requirements must also be checked. An EU VAT number is not proof of a local establishment.

For a foreign producer subject to Belgian packaging EPR, Fost Plus says a non-Belgian company without a Belgian establishment must mandate an EPR authorised representative for producer registration. Valipac confirms the representative must be established in Belgium; a Belgian VAT number alone is not enough. Confirm the producer and sales route, including any platform filing arrangement, before assigning tasks.

Preparing from the US or UK?

Use the starting point for your selling entity: EU EPR compliance for US companies or EU EPR compliance for UK sellers. Each page sets out the sales routes, existing records and service scope to prepare before discussing registration and representation.

What to prepare

  1. Legal company name, registered address and company registration details.
  2. Product categories, packaging materials and component weights.
  3. Sales channels, Belgian customers and annual market quantities.
  4. Existing registrations, scheme memberships and representation mandates.

When is the Belgian packaging declaration due?

If you already report Belgian packaging through Fost Plus or Valipac, first identify which packaging your membership covers.

  • Household packaging. Fost Plus members report the household packaging they placed on the Belgian market in the previous calendar year by 28 February. Submit through MyFost. Fost Plus declaration guidance.
  • Commercial and industrial packaging. If you have delegated these obligations to Valipac, its annual declaration of the packaging you are responsible for is also due by 28 February. Valipac client guidance.

For a US or UK business selling into several EU countries, start with Belgian shipments only. Reconcile the reporting year’s quantities with your records of packaging materials and weights. If a warehouse packs orders for you, ask which boxes and fillers it added. Use the worked packaging-weight example to organise those figures.

These dates cover the two Belgian scheme declarations. Check initial registration and marketplace evidence requests separately. Sources reviewed 4 November 2026.

Belgium EPR questions

Is GPSR included in EPR?

No. GPSR is separately scoped and quoted. A PPWR EPR mandate does not appoint a GPSR responsible person.

Does Fost Plus membership equal a producer-register number?

Do not treat them as interchangeable. Confirm which organisation issued the number, which entity and waste stream it covers, and the purpose for which it is requested.

Are packaging, WEEE and batteries one registration?

They are separate waste streams. A business selling electronics with batteries may need more than one compliance route.
