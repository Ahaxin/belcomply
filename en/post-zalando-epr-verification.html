Zalando EPR Verification: Belgium zDirect Requirements

By BelComply · Published and sources checked: 18 October 2026

What should you check first?

Identify the sales country, product category and legal selling entity before submitting evidence in the platform’s requested fields. Platform acceptance and legal compliance are separate checks; one number does not cover every market and stream.

The platform’s published requirements

Packaging: enter the legal entity name and VAT/Tax ID matching Fost Plus records, and tick the packaging compliance declaration. Do not substitute a battery or electronics number.

Batteries: Bebat client number, entity name and VAT/Tax ID. Electronics: Recupel member code, entity name and VAT/Tax ID; Zalando says entry is required from 31 October 2026.

In zDirect: Business profile → EPR details → Manage. Select the market and supplier type. Check validation status and correct mismatched records, including stray spaces.

Official zDirect submission guide

Your evidence checklist before and after submission

  1. Save the account notice and date; confirm the country, category and selling entity it concerns.
  2. Match names, identifiers and scope against authentic registration or scheme records. Never borrow another company’s evidence.
  3. Prepare packaging materials, weights and sales destinations; distinguish product packaging from fulfilment-added shipping packaging.
  4. Keep submission status and confirmation records. Correct source-record mismatches before resubmitting through the platform workflow.

Do these requirements apply to every seller?

These summaries concern the countries, categories and business roles described by the official page. Account access and notices can differ. An unpublished rollout date should not become a universal deadline. Assess packaging, batteries and electronics separately; GPSR is a separate product-safety service.
