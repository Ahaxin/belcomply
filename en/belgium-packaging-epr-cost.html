How much does packaging EPR cost in Belgium in 2027?

Belgium packaging costs depend on the applicable scheme, material classification and amount placed on the market. Fost Plus publishes household packaging rates per kilogram; Valipac publishes commercial and industrial packaging rates per tonne. Choose the applicable route before comparing rates. The two schemes are not interchangeable.

Check the producer, destination and packaging stream with your Belgium EPR registration assessment. Separate packaging scheme contributions from professional registration, reporting and PPWR EPR representative service fees.

Worked calculation: what does the estimate include?

Assume you place 1,000 kg of packaging correctly classified as Fost Plus paper/cardboard and 100 kg of eligible PE film in Belgium during 2027. The variable calculation is:

1,000 × €0.1503 + 100 × €1.2721 = €277.51. This is the illustrative weight-based component, before other charges or service fees. For 2026, the same weights and eligible classifications would give €258.34.

For a separate industrial example, 2,000 kg of Valipac recyclable non-plastic packaging at the 2027 rate of €25.50/tonne gives €51.00 before extra charges. The published Valipac minimum financing contribution is €50; it is not included in the raw calculator result.

Which fees are outside this estimate?

  • Fost Plus minimum or fixed contributions, applicable litter levy, VAT and other charges. Some categories use different rates or simplified methods.
  • Valipac minimum financing contribution, regional tax and producer registration fee. Its 2027 regulator-charge table lists some amounts as pending for under 3 tonnes; pending does not mean zero. The published regulator table uses its stated historical tonnage reference, so do not derive it from this calculator's input.
  • Registration, reporting, representative mandate and renewal service fees; these are separately scoped. Read how to compare Belgian EPR representative quotes.
  • Other countries, WEEE, batteries and GPSR. This calculator covers selected Belgian packaging fees only.

What should a US or UK seller prepare?

Record the actual legal producer, sales route, household or commercial stream, components and material weights for the Belgian market. Use a separate row for each material and year. Our packaging data worksheet example explains how to gather these quantities. Ask scheme staff to confirm classification if you are unsure.
