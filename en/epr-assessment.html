BELCOMPLY / EU EPR

Free initial EPR assessment for EU sellers

BelComply offers a free initial assessment to understand your packaging, electrical-equipment and battery EPR needs. Share your company location, products and EU sales countries. We use that information to identify the service scope and missing inputs before preparing a separate quote for professional work.

Request a free initial assessment

What does the free assessment cover?

The discussion starts with the legal selling entity, the products and the countries where those products are sold. We distinguish packaging, electrical equipment and batteries rather than treating EPR as a single universal registration. The aim is to identify the information needed for a proposal and the service lines to consider. The free step does not itself create a registration, submit a declaration or establish an authorised-representative relationship.

Which information should you send?

Provide the company name and country of establishment, the EU markets you sell into, selling channels, product descriptions and estimated quantities. Add existing registration records and the text of any marketplace request. For packaging, prepare materials and weights. For electrical equipment, provide models and intended use. For batteries, state whether they are standalone or incorporated into a device. Approximate planning data should be marked as estimates rather than final reporting figures.

Which countries and representative services can be discussed?

Start with Belgium, Germany, France, Italy or the Netherlands, and include any additional EU destination you are planning. Compliance support is scoped by market. AR services can be discussed for Belgium, Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands, with category-specific eligibility and written responsibilities checked separately. A company based in the US, UK, China, the Netherlands, Poland or Czechia should state its actual establishment and sales route; the company location and service country are different facts.

What happens after you contact BelComply?

Use the contact form or write to info@belcomply.com. The website contact flow opens an email draft; you still need to send it from your mail application. BelComply reviews the information and discusses the missing details needed to define an engagement. If you request paid implementation, the proposal separates professional tasks from external charges and identifies what you supply. There is no mandatory purchase of further services after the free assessment.

Does the assessment guarantee approval?

No. The relevant register, scheme or marketplace controls its own decisions. An initial assessment identifies the questions and preparation work; it is not proof that a registration has been accepted. A proposal should make dependencies visible, including your company records, product data and any written mandate. Product-safety or GPSR responsible-person work is a separate service line and should not be inferred from an EPR assessment.
