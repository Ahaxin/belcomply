Etsy EPR requirements: registration numbers and EU packaging

By BelComply · Published and sources checked: 21 November 2026

Do Etsy sellers need an EPR registration number?

If you sell physical goods to EU customers as a business and qualify as a packaging producer in the destination country, you may need packaging registration, reporting, scheme membership and an authorised representative. Etsy's September 2026 handbook identifies France, Germany and Spain as the markets where it was collecting EPR numbers, with further countries to follow. Belgian packaging obligations need a separate assessment; do not assume Etsy already has a Belgian submission field.

Etsy Seller Handbook, 3 September 2026 · PPWR, Regulation (EU) 2025/40

Which EPR numbers does Etsy ask for?

The country and product category determine the actual registration record. One number is not valid for every waste stream or market. The following table summarises public Etsy guidance and the relevant authorities checked on 21 November 2026. Account availability may differ.

DestinationRegistration evidence and platform status
GermanyPackaging: LUCID registration from ZSVR; WEEE and batteries have separate registration records with Stiftung EAR when relevant. Etsy's Germany help specifies the country registration fields. Check the applicable recycling-system obligations separately.
FranceADEME's unique identification number (IDU/UIN) for each relevant EPR scheme. Etsy's France help also covers additional product categories, such as textiles or toys, and says the number should be visible to buyers. Check the specific category instead of using the packaging number for all products.
SpainPackaging: the packaging section of the Product Producer Register (RPP) administered by MITECO. Electrical equipment and batteries have distinct registers, RII-AEE and RII-PYA respectively. See Etsy's Spain help.
BelgiumNational packaging EPR applies according to the producer's role and waste stream. The cited Etsy handbook does not list a Belgian EPR number collection field. Verify any actual account notice or current platform arrangement before submission.
Other EU countriesCheck the producer's national obligations and whether Etsy has opened a relevant evidence workflow. An unlisted field is not a legal exemption.

National sources: ZSVR: changes since 12 August 2026 · ADEME IDU/UIN · MITECO packaging register. See our EPR registration number guide to check the issuing body, entity and category.

How to add your EPR number in Etsy Shop Manager

  1. Open your shop's management area (Shop Manager) and select Finances.
  2. Choose Legal and tax information and locate Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Registrations.
  3. Use Add info under the applicable country. Match the actual registration record to the country and category requested.
  4. Follow the current screen's certification and saving instructions; review any declaration before confirming it. Keep the recorded information, date and subsequent platform notice together.

These steps follow Etsy's published Help Center and Handbook. We have not tested the workflow using a live seller account. Registration, reporting and payment obligations remain separate from adding data in Shop Manager. France's Etsy help also instructs sellers to display the relevant number in a buyer-visible location, such as the shop's policies or buyer message; Germany and Spain guidance refers to providing recycling and return information where applicable.

What if you sell to Belgium on Etsy?

A US or UK shop shipping packaged goods directly to Belgian end users should first assess whether it is a producer for those packaging quantities. Distinguish household packaging (Fost Plus) from professional packaging (Valipac) instead of assuming one membership fits every stream.

Fost Plus explains the foreign producer and representation requirements and notes that some platform-only registration arrangements may operate through the marketplace. Valipac likewise advises sellers to check whether their marketplace actually offers to perform declarations. Its current FAQ anticipates the Belgian producer register at the end of 2026 or early 2027 and distinguishes a membership reference from a producer registration number. This is an expected period, not a confirmed opening date.

We have not found public Etsy confirmation of a Belgian EPR entry or a Belgian Etsy pay-on-behalf arrangement in the official sources reviewed. Do not infer that one exists, or that missing fields remove your responsibility. Verify how the particular selling route is handled, what documentation is accepted, and whether an applicable PPWR EPR mandate is necessary.

Do US and UK Etsy shops need an authorised representative?

It depends on the country, producer role, establishment and applicable category. For example, ZSVR describes the local-authorised-representative requirement for overseas businesses without a German branch selling empty packaging or packaged goods directly to German end users from 12 August 2026. The German producer still has to complete its own LUCID registration. Etsy also describes representation for qualifying foreign producers in Spain. The appointment and registration route are country-specific, not a single EU licence.

For small craft sellers, do not assume handmade goods, low sales volume or a micro-enterprise status creates a blanket EU exemption. The EU reform on EPR representative rules remains under legislative consideration at the review date. Check the current applicable rules before relying on proposed simplifications. Start with our representative eligibility guide, the US seller route or the UK seller route.

Example: preparing packaging weight records for a craft shop

Suppose a UK jewellery shop makes 100 direct shipments to German customers in a reporting period, with one 25 g gift box and one 15 g paper mailing envelope per order. The shop's illustrative weight totals are as follows. Actual reporting depends on the accepted material classifications, relevant producer duties and complete sales data.

Packaging componentIllustrative calculation
Gift box100 × 25 g = 2,500 g = 2.5 kg
Paper mailing envelope100 × 15 g = 1,500 g = 1.5 kg
Combined recorded weight4 kg; record the material breakdown rather than filing as an undifferentiated total.

Include packing inserts, labels or fillers where applicable. Keep Belgian and French shipments in their own country records, and distinguish actual quantities from estimates. This example does not establish a threshold, exemption, bill or confirmed registration obligation for the shop.

What should you prepare before contacting a compliance provider?

  • Legal selling entity and actual establishment; EU destination markets and sales channels.
  • Product descriptions and categories, including relevant electronics, batteries or other EPR streams.
  • Packaging components, materials, weights, quantities and destination breakdown.
  • Existing registrations, scheme participation, declarations and any written representative mandates.
  • The exact Etsy EPR notice, country, category and requested evidence; do not substitute a VAT number for an EPR number.

EPR is not GPSR. Packaging and product EPR concern waste responsibilities. The Etsy GPSR guidance concerns a different product-safety regime. GPSR support is separately scoped and quoted by BelComply, not included in a PPWR packaging EPR appointment.
