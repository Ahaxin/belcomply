EPR Registration Number: What It Is & Where to Find It

Updated and sources reviewed 27 November 2026

An EPR registration number identifies a producer or registration record for a specific country and EPR category. There is no universal EU EPR number. Before giving a number to Amazon, eBay or another marketplace, verify the legal entity, country, waste stream and issuing body against the original record.

Selling from the UK? Domestic packaging EPR records are not a substitute for an EU country registration number. See EU EPR registration and representation for UK sellers, or our EU EPR service for US companies, to map your destination and selling route.

Choose your next step: Apply for an EPR number · Find or check an existing number · Prepare marketplace evidence.

How do I get an EPR number if I do not have one?

  1. Identify the obligation. Confirm the producer, each destination country and the EPR categories that apply to the product and its packaging. If an online marketplace asks for a number, read the exact country and category in the notice.
  2. Find the correct registration route. Use the official register or applicable scheme for that country and category. For packaging, see the country-by-country producer register guide. Confirm whether a written mandate or a local Belgian packaging EPR authorised representative is relevant before applying in Belgium.
  3. Keep the real confirmation. Obtain the registration decision or official identifier through the applicable route, verify that it matches your legal entity and retain any separate scheme participation, reporting and mandate records. A registration number by itself is not proof that every obligation is fulfilled.

If you have not yet identified the required register or category, request a free initial EPR assessment. BelComply can help scope the formal registration work separately; neither an assessment nor a submitted application is a registration number.

Belgium EPR registration number: Fost Plus, Valipac and EPRiBEL

Fost Plus explains that it arranges producer registration collectively for its members and makes the authority-confirmed number available in MyFost. Valipac states that its membership number and producer-register number are different, and expects the Belgian producer register to become available at the end of 2026 or early 2027. That is its published expectation, not a confirmed launch date or evidence that an individual registration has been issued.

If the authority has not yet confirmed your registration, retain the genuine membership or application documents and ask the relevant organisation or platform which evidence is accepted for your situation. Do not call a pending application a completed registration. Review the Belgium packaging EPR route and whether a written PPWR EPR representative mandate is needed for your producer and sales route.

What if I sell on Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Zalando or bol?

Start with the exact marketplace request, rather than searching for an interchangeable EPR number. Note the seller account, country, category, requested evidence type, issuer and legal entity. Only enter the identifier requested in the applicable official workflow. A VAT number is not a substitute for EPR evidence.

See our platform guides for Amazon EPR verification, eBay, Etsy, Zalando and bol. Each platform may request different evidence, depending on the market and category.

Example: a seller with records from two countries

For an illustrative US business selling to Germany and Belgium, keep one evidence row per market and waste stream. The German packaging row may be confirmed by a LUCID record, while the Belgian packaging row could still be awaiting the producer-register confirmation. The two rows cannot be completed with the same number simply because they concern the same company.

If the platform rejects your EPR number

  1. Keep the exact rejection and the original evidence.
  2. Check the country, category, number type and named legal entity.
  3. Ask the issuer or platform which field or document is missing, and correct the underlying record if needed.
  4. Follow the current account instructions when you resubmit; retain the outcome.
