GPSR / PRODUCT SAFETY

GPSR & EU responsible-person support

Product safety is a separate business from packaging EPR. Get a scope tailored to your consumer products.

Is a PPWR authorised representative the same as a GPSR responsible person?

No. They arise from different regulatory duties. An EPR representative mandate concerns producer responsibility for packaging waste. A GPSR responsible-person arrangement concerns applicable consumer-product safety duties. Each appointment needs its own scope.

Start with the product and economic operator

Share your product type, manufacturer details, target EU countries and existing product documents. We review the relevant role and scope before proposing a GPSR service. Product-specific legislation may also apply.

What we need to scope the service

  • Product description, intended use and images.
  • Manufacturer and importer details, where applicable.
  • Available safety assessments, labels, instructions and traceability information.
  • EU sales channels and current responsible-person arrangements.

A separate scope and quotation

GPSR services are not included in EPR registration or PPWR representation. Deliverables, exclusions and fees are confirmed before engagement. No certification, testing outcome or marketplace approval is implied.
