Selling DTC into the EU: EPR for independent stores

12 Oct 2026 · DTC

Marketplace sellers are pushed into EPR compliance by platform verification — a listing simply will not go live without a registration number. DTC brands shipping from their own storefront have no such gate. But the obligation is identical, and enforcement comes from a different direction.

TL;DR — A DTC brand shipping from its own store is the producer under the PPWR, with the same registration and authorised-representative obligations as any marketplace seller — enforced directly by national authorities rather than a platform.

You are the producer, with no intermediary

When you sell through your own store — Shopify, WooCommerce, or any platform where you are the merchant of record — there is no marketplace and no importer standing between you and the EU consumer. You place the packaged product on the market directly, which makes you the producer in every sense of the PPWR.

The compliance gap DTC sellers carry

Marketplace sellers get a visible enforcement trigger: the platform asks for a number, and if you cannot produce it, your listing is blocked. DTC sellers do not see that. Your first warning is more likely a letter from a national authority, or a customs stop at the border, or an audit triggered by a competitor — by which point you are already non-compliant and facing penalties, not a friendly reminder.

What DTC compliance looks like

  • Register as a producer in each country where you sell directly to consumers.
  • Appoint an authorised representative in each member state where you are not established.
  • Join the producer responsibility organisation and pay eco-fees on the packaging you ship.
  • File annual declarations of the volumes you placed on the market.

Why the same partner model works

The advantage for a DTC brand is that there is no platform imposing its own checklist — you choose your own compliance path. One coordinating partner can handle the authorised representatives and registrations across every country you ship to, so you deal with a single contact instead of a stack of national providers.

The practical takeaway: a DTC store does not soften the EPR obligation — it removes the early-warning system. Start your registration before you ship volume, because your first compliance signal may be a penalty, not a prompt.

Sources: PPWR (EU) 2025/40.
