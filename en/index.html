Compliance duty

Do you actually need an EU authorised representative?

The test is simple: is your packaging placed on the market of an EU member state, delivered directly to an end consumer in that country, by a company without a local entity? If so, you are the producer — and the law requires you to appoint an authorised representative in that member state. There is no minimum volume: the obligation applies from the very first package.

Your sales modelAR required?Why
Amazon FBA (Pan-EU / EFN), goods reaching consumers in an EU member state Yes Foreign company delivers directly to end users → you are the producer
Own website / eBay / bol.com shipping direct to an EU member state Yes Same logic — direct distance selling to consumers in that country
Selling to an EU wholesaler / importer No The importer becomes the producer; the obligation shifts to them
You already have a legal entity in that EU member state No You can register directly — no authorised representative needed

Your legal status: under PPWR (EU) 2025/40 Article 45, a producer not established in the member state where it places packaging on the market must appoint an authorised representative established in that member state. Belgium applied this earlier — since 3 Sep 2020, a foreign company selling directly to Belgian consumers must appoint an AR (Type B packaging producer, the word « désigne » = "must appoint").
