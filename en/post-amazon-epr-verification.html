Amazon EPR Verification: Registration Numbers & Evidence

Updated and sources reviewed 21 November 2026

Amazon EPR verification depends on the selling country, EPR category and legal seller. There is no single EPR number that covers every EU market. Match the registration or other evidence to your actual Seller Central notice before submitting it.

Amazon Belgium EPR registration: what should sellers prepare?

For Belgian packaging, first identify the producer, sales route and packaging type. Fost Plus describes collective registration for members, with the official number made available after authority confirmation. Valipac distinguishes membership from the future producer-register number. Keep genuine scheme or registration evidence and use the exact information requested for your account.

The Commission's current guidance distinguishes existing national obligations from the new harmonised producer-register rollout. It says platforms are not yet generally required to collect the new numbers through that framework until the harmonised registers are in place. This does not cancel existing country, product or marketplace obligations.

Fost Plus registration · Valipac FAQ · Commission packaging EPR guidance. See Belgium packaging EPR services for your registration and representation route.

How do I respond to an Amazon verification notice?

  1. Open the notice. Identify the destination market, marketplace account, category and any stated deadline.
  2. Find the correct record. Determine if the requested field concerns a producer-register number, a membership reference or another document.
  3. Match the entity. Compare the account and original record, including any brand or legal-name difference.
  4. Use the current Seller Central instructions. Amazon's public guide points to its EPR compliance information page for German packaging and French EPR UINs. Account navigation can change; follow the current notice rather than an unverified menu path.
  5. Save the result. Retain the uploaded document, submission date, confirmation or exact rejection. A submitted file is not automatically accepted.

Why might an EPR number be rejected?

Check for a wrong waste stream, issuing country, company name or record type; a permanent registration and a pending application are different. Do not alter evidence or borrow another seller's number. If a platform status is unclear, check the underlying document and ask the issuer or platform what needs to be corrected.

Does FBA change packaging EPR responsibilities?

FBA is a fulfilment arrangement. Distinguish product packaging from shipping materials added by a fulfilment service, establish who first makes the packaging available in each destination, and review the applicable national rules. A fulfilment agreement alone does not settle all EPR duties. See the FBA packaging EPR guide.
