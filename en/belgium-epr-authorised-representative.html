BELGIUM / PPWR EPR REPRESENTATION

Belgium packaging EPR authorised representative

BelComply, based in Belgium, helps US, UK and other overseas businesses assess and arrange Belgian packaging EPR representation under PPWR. We scope the written mandate, applicable registration route and reporting responsibilities with you before you commit.

Need an authorised representative (AR) for Belgian packaging?

If your business is a foreign producer subject to Belgian packaging EPR obligations, current Belgian scheme guidance calls for a representative established in Belgium. The appointment concerns packaging EPR in the agreed scope; it does not create a GPSR responsible-person appointment.

When should overseas sellers discuss an AR appointment?

The term authorised representative is also written as authorized representative in US English. Start by identifying the actual producer and the country in which it is established. A US or UK company selling packaged goods online directly to Belgian end users is a common situation to assess. A company supplying a Belgian distributor, selling from local stock or using a marketplace may have a different allocation of obligations. A VAT registration alone does not establish a local company presence.

Fost Plus explains that non-Belgian producers without a Belgian establishment must appoint an EPR representative for producer registration; Valipac confirms the requirement for a Belgian-established representative for foreign producers subject to the Belgian obligations. If you sell through a platform, confirm which declarations it actually handles and which obligations remain with your business. See our guide to who needs a written EPR mandate for the applicable criteria.

Official references reviewed 21 November 2026: PPWR, Articles 44 and 45 · Fost Plus: applicable PPWR rules · Valipac FAQ: foreign producers and ARs. Proposed changes to EU cross-border AR requirements remain under legislative consideration; they should not be treated as an effective exemption.

What should the written mandate and service proposal cover?

Registration support, annual reporting and an authorised-representative appointment are different tasks. We discuss them together, but specify the agreed responsibilities separately. Use the following checklist to compare service proposals and confirm the actual scope before signing.

Producer and appointmentLegal entity, Belgian packaging stream, applicable duties, start date, term and written mandate.
Registration and scheme routeWho prepares the relevant application and handles correspondence; retain the actual registration or membership evidence when available.
Annual declarationsReporting period, material and weight data, review and submission responsibilities, and evidence of any completed declaration.
Payments and changesProfessional fees versus scheme contributions, who pays them, and how changes to company details, volumes or the mandate are handled.
Handover or renewalNotice, renewal and document handover arrangements; confirm which responsibilities remain with the producer.

The table describes the points to agree, not a promise that every action is included in every appointment. Your own proposal determines the tasks, documents, exclusions and fees for your situation.

How does an engagement start?

  1. Initial scope: share your company location, products, sales route and Belgian destinations. The initial needs assessment is free.
  2. Route and evidence: identify household or commercial/industrial packaging, existing memberships, registration records and any marketplace request.
  3. Written proposal: review the country, stream, mandate duties, professional fees, external charges and data responsibilities.
  4. Agreed work: after acceptance, prepare the agreed steps and retain the evidence actually issued or submitted. Decisions and processing times depend on the relevant organisations.

What information will help you prepare a useful quote?

  • Legal seller name, country of establishment, company registration and VAT details, if applicable.
  • Belgian sales channels: your own website, marketplace, distributor or local fulfilment; identify the contracting seller.
  • Products, packaging components, material weights, quantities and reporting year.
  • Existing Fost Plus or Valipac documents, producer-register records and any current mandate.
  • Exact marketplace requests, including the country, waste stream and evidence field.

Use our EPR registration number guide to distinguish a registration record, scheme reference and mandate. An application receipt is not proof of acceptance.

Questions from US and UK businesses

Does a Belgian AR cover all EU countries?

No automatic extension applies. BelComply supports compliance across the EU, while each market and waste stream needs its own applicable scope. See our EU coverage and EU packaging EPR services for multi-country planning.

Is a US or UK legal entity eligible for an assessment?

Yes. Tell us your actual establishment and how goods reach Belgian customers. Our US company and UK seller guides explain which records to prepare. An assessment does not itself establish a formal appointment.

Does the PPWR EPR appointment include GPSR?

No. GPSR product-safety and EU responsible-person support is a separate service. WEEE and battery EPR are also distinct from a packaging mandate.

Can BelComply guarantee a marketplace will accept my evidence?

No. A marketplace or scheme controls its own verification. BelComply is listed in the Amazon Service Provider Network; that directory listing is not a guarantee of registration, verification or approval. Check the specific evidence requested by the marketplace.
