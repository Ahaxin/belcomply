Country coverage and registration are different

Our EU-wide capability does not mean that one registration or number automatically covers every country. We identify the country, waste stream and responsible company for each part of the plan. Tell us about new destinations before relying on an existing arrangement.

MARKETPLACE COMPLIANCE

What marketplaces actually check

Official public guidance checked on 17 October 2026. These are platform workflows, not a single EU legal rule. Match your account notice, product category and destination country before submitting.

Zalando · zDirect

Belgium: packaging, batteries & electronics

Packaging: enter the legal entity name and VAT/Tax ID matching Fost Plus records, and tick the packaging compliance declaration. Do not substitute a battery or electronics number.

Batteries: Bebat client number, entity name and VAT/Tax ID. Electronics: Recupel member code, entity name and VAT/Tax ID; Zalando says entry is required from 31 October 2026.

In zDirect: Business profile → EPR details → Manage. Select the market and supplier type. Check validation status and correct mismatched records, including stray spaces.

Official zDirect submission guide

bol · Partnerplatform

Netherlands & Belgium: separate EPR checks

bol lists sales-rights checks for selected Belgian categories including electronics, batteries, tyres, mattresses and solar panels, plus batteries in the Netherlands.

Evidence depends on your role: a purchase invoice if not liable, scheme-enrolment proof for liable non-EU partners, or proof of individual contributions. bol identifies non-EU partners without a VAT number as needing a separate application rather than automatic approval.

Packaging: bol asks sellers to check registrations and gather material data. Its public PPWR page says large-scale registration checks are not yet active and does not provide a universal upload field or start date. bol handles the bol-branded shipping packaging it adds through Logistics via bol; sellers retain responsibility for their item packaging according to their role.

Official EPR sales-rights requirements · Official packaging / PPWR guidance

Amazon · Seller Central

Germany & France: country and category matter

Amazon’s public guide asks for a 13-digit LUCID number for German packaging and relevant UINs for French EPR categories through its EPR compliance portal. German electronics follow a separate registration route.

Check the country, category, selling entity and notice in your account. Do not reuse these fields as a Belgian requirement: the public guide does not establish one universal EU submission process.

Official Amazon EPR guide

eBay · Business information

Packaging: submit country by country

On a computer or tablet, open Account Settings → Business Info → EPR registration numbers → Add or Manage. Select the country and Packaging category, enter the number and tick the compliance declaration before adding it. Repeat for each relevant country.

eBay says existing French and German packaging numbers are migrated automatically; update them if inaccurate. An entry in your account is not a replacement for the underlying registration.

Official eBay packaging guidance

Prepare the platform notice, selling entity, markets, categories and existing registration evidence. We can help review the documents and service scope; the marketplace decides acceptance. GPSR remains a separate service.
