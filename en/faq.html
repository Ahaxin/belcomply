EPR / FAQ

Belgium & EU EPR: your questions answered

Practical answers for US, UK and Chinese businesses. Registration, PPWR representation and GPSR are kept distinct.

Do you cover every EU country?

We support EPR compliance across all 27 EU member states, with a core focus on Belgium. Each country and waste stream is scoped separately. Coverage does not mean one registration or one mandate replaces every national requirement.

Is GPSR included with EPR?

No. GPSR is separately scoped and quoted. A PPWR EPR representative mandate concerns producer-responsibility duties; a GPSR responsible-person arrangement concerns applicable product-safety duties.

Who needs a Belgian EPR authorised representative?

Assess the producer’s establishment, sales route, customer type and waste stream against the rules applying in Belgium. PPWR representation and existing national duties must be read together. A VAT registration is not the same as an establishment.

Are US, UK and Chinese companies assessed in the same way?

Their overseas status is only part of the assessment. The responsible producer, target country, product and sales route still matter. UK-specific or Northern Ireland arrangements may require an additional factual review.

Does selling through Amazon FBA remove EPR obligations?

Do not assume that it does. Identify the relevant producer and distinguish your product packaging from shipping packaging added by the fulfilment provider. Check the country and applicable stream.

Is Fost Plus membership a producer-register number?

Do not use the terms interchangeably. Confirm the issuer, legal entity, waste stream and purpose of the number. Follow the current official registration route rather than assuming every register has the same status.

Does the 300 kg figure mean no obligations at all?

Do not use a threshold from one duty as a blanket exemption. Identify the year, country, stream and duty to which it applies, then check registration, representation and contributions separately against current guidance.

Are batteries and WEEE included in packaging registration?

They are separate streams. A product with a battery and packaging may trigger several obligations, each with its own classification, evidence and reporting route.

How much will registration cost?

We quote after reviewing your countries, entity, products and data. Professional fees, contributions and other external costs are separated. Any estimate should state its assumptions and rate year.

How long does registration take?

Timing depends on the route, complete documents, mandate where required and the relevant organisation. Request a current estimate for your case; no fixed completion or marketplace-acceptance date is implied.

What should I prepare before asking for a quote?

Share the legal company name, registration details, country of establishment, product categories, sales channels, countries of sale and existing compliance documents. Packaging weight and volume data help define reporting work.

Can you explain the process in Chinese?

Yes. BelComply serves businesses in English and Chinese. We can discuss preparation and scope in either language; the required filing documents follow the relevant organisation’s process.
