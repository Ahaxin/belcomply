How we work

Direct where it matters, networked where it's smarter

EPR is not one regime — it is a patchwork of national rules. We are honest about which part we run ourselves and which part we coordinate, so you always know who is accountable.

Direct service

We are established in Belgium and handle the full cycle ourselves: Fost Plus registration, the authorisation filed with EPRiBEL, annual declarations, eco-fees and ongoing correspondence. No middleman.

  • ✓Direct line to EPRiBEL, Fost Plus, Valipac, Recupel & Bebat
  • ✓4–6 weeks registration — one of the fastest in the EU
  • ✓Liability taken on by our own Belgian entity
Partner network

For every other EU member state we coordinate through a vetted network of local compliance partners. You deal with us — one contact, one quote, one accountable party — while the in-country filing is done by a licensed local specialist.
