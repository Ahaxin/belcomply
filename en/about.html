About BelComply

BelComply is a Belgium-based compliance business serving companies in the United States, United Kingdom and China. We help you prepare EPR registration, PPWR authorised representation and reporting across all 27 EU member states. Communication is available in English and Chinese.

What we help you organise

Your products may involve packaging, electrical equipment or batteries. We start by separating the relevant streams, checking the selling entity and mapping the destinations. Registration records, representative mandates and reporting data are treated as distinct tasks.

Two service lines, clearly scoped

EPR authorised representation concerns PPWR obligations within the agreed scope. GPSR product-safety and EU responsible-person support are separate services and are not included in EPR. The proposal should state what is included, what information you must provide and any external contributions.
