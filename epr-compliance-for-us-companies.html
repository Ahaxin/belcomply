The obligation

This is not optional, and it is already live

Three facts decide whether your US company is affected — and most e-commerce and export businesses hit all three.

You are the producer

If your packaged products reach EU consumers directly — through Amazon FBA, your own store, or any marketplace — you are the "producer" under the PPWR, even with no EU entity.

One representative per country

There is no EU-wide single representative. Each member state requires its own authorised representative, established in that country, under a written mandate.

Marketplaces verify first

Amazon, eBay and Etsy check EPR registration numbers before letting you list. No number means suspended listings — often before any fine arrives.
