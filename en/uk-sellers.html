UK packaging EPR, EU EPR and PPWR: what is the difference?

For a business in Great Britain selling to customers in Europe, UK packaging EPR (pEPR), EU producer responsibilities and the PPWR packaging regulation must be considered separately. Requirements can overlap, but proof from one regime does not automatically satisfy another.

FrameworkWhat a UK seller should check
UK packaging EPRAssess domestic producer duties under the UK rules and retain applicable reporting/registration records. Do not transfer UK thresholds or registrations into an EU assessment.
EU packaging EPRDetermine which business is the producer for packaging in each destination. Check the relevant producer register, permitted scheme route, contributions, declarations and applicable representative requirement.
EU PPWRRegulation (EU) 2025/40 generally applies from 12 August 2026, with further requirements phased in. Separate packaging design, documentation and other obligations from the EPR appointment and reporting scope.

Sources reviewed 21 November 2026: GOV.UK packaging EPR eligibility · UK government guidance for EU exporters · PPWR Articles 44–45.

Which UK-to-EU selling route applies to your business?

Start with the legal seller, the country where the goods go and the parties responsible for the packaging. These examples help identify the information we need for a scoped EPR assessment.

Typical UK business situationWhat to establish before registration
Direct parcels from Great Britain to Belgian consumersIdentify the contracting UK seller, whether it is the producer and both product and shipping packaging. Check Belgian scheme and representative requirements before deciding how to submit evidence.
Amazon FBA or EU fulfilment warehouseMap stock locations, the entity making products available in each country, actual sales destinations and who adds each packaging component. Do not infer compliance from an Amazon account or a VAT number.

Do UK exporters need an EPR authorised representative in the EU?

A PPWR EPR representative is a country-specific arrangement, not a single licence for all EU markets. Article 45 and applicable national rules determine when appointment is required. For a qualifying overseas producer with no Belgian establishment, current Fost Plus and Valipac guidance calls for a Belgian-established representative. Verify the written mandate and the actual registration route, including any platform reporting arrangement.

Germany's ZSVR guidance describes a local representative requirement for in-scope businesses without a German branch selling packaging or packaged goods directly to German end users. The producer itself remains responsible for completing its LUCID registration. Other markets must be assessed under their own rules; the proposed EU changes to representative requirements are still under legislative consideration and are not a blanket exemption at the review date.

Prepare your UK-to-EU assessment in one set of documents

  • UK business: company registration details, registered address and any existing UK packaging records.
  • EU footprint: direct and marketplace sales, EU warehouses or distributors, legal seller and quantities by destination.
  • Packaging data: item and shipping packaging material, component weight and supplier or fulfilment records; separate data for relevant equipment and batteries.
  • Existing evidence: national registration numbers, membership confirmations, filings and mandates; keep the exact notices from all marketplaces.

For example, a UK shop with 120 Belgian and 80 German orders in a reporting period should keep two destination records, not count all 200 orders under its UK registration. These are illustrative quantities, not a legal threshold. Use the packaging-data worksheet and EPR-number evidence checklist for the actual records.

UK seller questions

Can a UK VAT or EORI number be used as an EU EPR number?

No. Tax or customs identifiers serve different purposes. Verify the country-specific EPR registration record and legal entity against the requested waste stream.

Does one Belgian representative cover other EU countries?

Not automatically. The national requirement and appointment scope must be agreed separately for each applicable market. BelComply can help plan EU-wide compliance while retaining country-specific responsibilities.

Are small UK shops automatically exempt?

No general exemption should be inferred from a small order count, a UK threshold or micro-enterprise status. Check the applicable EU and national rules for each producer and destination before relying on an exception.

Does PPWR representation include GPSR?

No. GPSR product-safety and EU responsible-person support is a separate service. Packaging EPR, WEEE and batteries must also be scoped independently.
