EU EPR compliance for Chinese businesses

Based in Belgium, with EPR support across all 27 EU member states.

Where should you start?

Map your selling entity, product categories, sales channels and destination countries. These facts determine who is the producer and which packaging, WEEE or battery obligations need attention. Overseas status alone does not replace this assessment.

Chinese company documents, usable packaging data

Prepare the legal company name in its original form, registration identifier, registered address and an English working version where requested. Keep the name consistent across registrations, mandates and marketplace records. Ask suppliers for component-level weights for cartons, film, labels and protective materials; product weight is not packaging weight. Split the quantities by EU destination country instead of relying on one total for Europe.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Company registration and registered business address.
  2. Countries of sale, marketplaces and direct-to-consumer channels.
  3. Product categories, packaging materials, weights and annual country-level volumes.
  4. Existing EPR registrations, scheme memberships and mandates.
