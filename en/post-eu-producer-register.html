PACKAGING EPR / REGISTRATION & REPRESENTATION

EU Packaging EPR Producer Registers by Country

Updated 21 November 2026 · official source review

There is no single EU-wide packaging EPR registration number. Check the producer register in each EU destination where your company has obligations. Belgium, Germany, France and Spain have different registration routes and evidence; a scheme membership alone may not confirm national registration.

For a UK exporter or US online seller, start with the country of sale, the responsible legal entity and the packaging stream. This guide points to the sources used to identify and check existing records, before deciding whether to register or update them.

Where to register and check packaging EPR by country

The table covers four common destinations, led by Belgium. The official sources provide the routes and evidence checks described below. Review the current instructions for your own producer role and packaging category; registration, scheme participation and representation are distinct tasks.

Belgium: what if I have a Fost Plus or Valipac membership but no producer-register number?

Start by identifying household packaging and industrial or commercial packaging. Fost Plus says it handles the national producer registration for members and will communicate the confirmed number through MyFost. Valipac says it will arrange registration for clients and that the national register identifier is distinct from its membership number. If you are not using a recognised scheme, Fost Plus points to direct registration through EPRiBEL (formerly IRPC/IVC-CIE); confirm the applicable route with the regulator. The EPRiBEL PPWR FAQ describes the register as being developed. As of this review, Valipac anticipates the Belgian register around the end of 2026 or beginning of 2027; this is its stated expectation, not a verified launch date or promise.

Keep your membership confirmation and ask the relevant scheme or authority which verification evidence is available now. If your company needs a locally established representative for Belgian packaging EPR, check the Belgium PPWR authorised representative service and confirm the mandate’s scope. Do not treat a GPSR representative appointment, Belgian VAT number or pending submission as the completed producer registration.

How to verify your existing registration before applying again

  1. Match the entity: compare your legal company name and identifier with the registration decision, public record or written confirmation.
  2. Match the country and stream: distinguish packaging from WEEE and batteries; check that the registration and scheme evidence cover the right activity.
  3. Confirm status: distinguish an assessment, submission receipt, official registration and scheme participation. Note the date of the official lookup or confirmation.
  4. Check changes: if your company, brands, packaging or sales route changed, confirm what must be updated. In Germany, ZSVR recommends restoring access to an existing registration instead of creating a duplicate.
  5. Keep a record: save the confirmation, any required separate scheme or AR documents, and unresolved items for the next reporting period.

For the record to send to a marketplace, see our EPR registration number and evidence guide. If you have no existing file, use the PPWR registration preparation checklist to collect legal-entity and packaging data.

Does PPWR harmonisation replace national EPR registrations?

No. The Commission says existing national registration requirements continue to apply while uniform registration and reporting rules are developed. A harmonised data format is not one registration number that covers all destinations. Check the operative national instructions rather than waiting for a future EU-wide number. European Commission guidance for small businesses.

Can one registration cover several marketplaces?

A registration belongs to the producer and the relevant country and stream, not simply to an Amazon, eBay or other shop account. If the same legal entity uses multiple sales channels, check that its registration and coverage match the sales activity and each platform’s own evidence request.
