US company, EU sales: map the transaction

A US company selling directly to EU end users should distinguish its own sales from supplies to an EU importer or distributor. Record who first makes the packaged product available in each market. A US company registration or an EU VAT registration alone does not decide the EPR role. For Amazon FBA, distinguish your product packaging from shipping packaging added by the fulfilment provider.

Prepare your US-to-EU sales brief

Describe these routes separately so the assessment reflects how your business actually sells:

  • Direct website orders: the US company named on the invoice, destination country and whether the customer is an end user.
  • Marketplace and fulfilment orders: the legal seller, stock locations, destination sales and packaging added by each party.
  • Wholesale shipments: the EU importer or distributor, contractual roles and the packaging supplied with the goods.

Keep packaging component weights in grams and sales quantities by destination. For a worked preparation example, use our US seller packaging worksheet guide.

Selling through Amazon, eBay, Zalando or bol?

Platform checks are a separate step from the underlying registration. Keep the platform notice and the issuing organisation’s evidence together. Use the guide that matches your channel: Amazon, eBay, Zalando or bol. Requirements depend on the country, category and request received.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Company registration and registered business address.
  2. Countries of sale, marketplaces and direct-to-consumer channels.
  3. Product categories, packaging materials, weights and annual country-level volumes.
  4. Existing EPR registrations, scheme memberships and mandates.
