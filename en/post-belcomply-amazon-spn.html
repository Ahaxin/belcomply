COMPANY UPDATE / AMAZON SPN

BelComply listed in Amazon Service Provider Network

Amazon sellers can find BelComply in the Compliance category of Amazon’s Service Provider Network (SPN). The listing gives sellers another way to find our EU EPR services and contact us about a defined scope.

What does the listing mean?

SPN is Amazon’s directory of independent third-party service providers. BelComply’s presence in the directory is not an Amazon endorsement of our services, and it does not guarantee acceptance of a seller’s EPR documents or marketplace account.

View BelComply’s SPN profile

Open our Compliance provider listing in Amazon Seller Central →

The direct link may ask you to sign in to Seller Central. The link supplied to us is set to a US seller and France selling-market view; those selections are browsing filters, not a statement that our service is limited to France. Check the countries and service details shown in your own Seller Central view before sending a request.

What can Amazon sellers discuss with BelComply?

Our website describes EU EPR support across all 27 member states, with Belgium as our home market. A consultation can cover packaging registration, reporting, marketplace evidence and PPWR EPR representation where applicable. We agree the country and waste stream separately for each engagement. GPSR product-safety support is a separate service.

Start with our Amazon EPR evidence guide if a marketplace notice asks for a number or document. The notice’s country, category and exact request determine which evidence to check.

Prepare your service request

  1. Identify the legal selling entity and its country of establishment.
  2. List the EU countries where you sell, including Belgium where relevant.
  3. Describe products, packaging, batteries or electrical equipment separately.
  4. Attach or quote the exact Amazon notice and any existing registration or scheme documents.

You can use the SPN profile or contact BelComply directly. Service terms, fees, duties and any representative mandate are agreed with BelComply, not with Amazon.
