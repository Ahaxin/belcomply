Accountability, from Belgium

BelComply helps Chinese and non-EU e-commerce sellers meet their Belgian packaging obligations — through a real Belgian entity that carries the responsibility.

Our mission

Why we do this

A non-EU producer is, practically speaking, beyond the reach of EU enforcement. The only realistic way to hold them to their Belgian packaging obligations is a named, locally accountable representative — one who assumes the same duties as the producer.

That is the role we fill. We are based in Belgium, we work directly with EPRiBEL, Fost Plus, Valipac, Recupel and Bebat, and we speak the two languages our clients actually use: Chinese and English.

What we stand for

  • ✓Responsibility — we carry the producer's obligations, not just paperwork
  • ✓Honesty — we tell you when you don't need an AR
  • ✓Locality — a Belgian entity, with direct lines to the authorities
How we work

Three commitments

An AR that just signs and disappears is worthless — and illegal. We stay in the loop for every declaration, every renewal, every enquiry.

Belgian compliance is settled in Belgium — with Belgian authorities, Belgian deadlines and Belgian enforcement. We're here, in the timezone and the system.
