Core service

Authorised Representative (AR)

We act as your authorised representative established in Belgium. By law we are subject to the same obligations as the packaging producer — that is the point: a non-EU seller cannot be reached by EU enforcement, but a named, locally accountable representative can.

What's included

  • ✓Fost Plus registration — your ERN number
  • ✓Power of attorney drafted & filed with EPRiBEL
  • ✓Annual packaging declaration (before 28 Feb)
  • ✓Eco-fee payment, passed through at cost
  • ✓Handling of official enquiries, audits & inspections
  • ✓Compliance declarations & technical-file custody
  • ✓Joint liability assumed by our Belgian entity

What's not included

  • ✗VAT registration & filing
  • ✗Customs clearance / EORI
  • ✗CE / EPREL / energy labelling
  • ✗Product-safety technical file (GPSR scope — included free)
  • ✗Other EPR streams (electronics / batteries / textiles — add-ons below)
Add-ons

Other EPR streams
