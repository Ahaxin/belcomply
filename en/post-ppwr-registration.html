PPWR Registration: Prepare Your Seller File

Updated 1 November 2026

Start a PPWR registration project with an assessed entity and country list, not with a promise of one application for all markets. The practical goal is a correct file and evidence of the completed steps.

Step 1: describe the business and transactions

Record the legal company, establishment, customer types, channels, destination countries and the first sales date relevant to the assessment. Separate direct sales to end users from supply to distributors. List existing records before deciding whether a new application is needed.

Step 2: build usable packaging data

Prepare component materials and weights, quantities by country, and the supporting specifications or calculations. Distinguish product packaging from materials added for dispatch. For Belgium, identify household and industrial/commercial packaging. Keep unresolved classification questions visible rather than filling a form with a guessed category.

Packaging data: a worked preparation example

Start with one row per packaging component, destination country and reporting period. Record the legal seller, SKU, sales channel, units per order, material, component weight, quantity and the source of the measurement. Keep supplier specifications or weighing records with their dates. This example assumes one product and one of each listed component per order.

Calculation: component grams per order × destination orders ÷ 1,000 = kilograms. The three listed components total 95 kg. This is invented arithmetic for preparation, not a customer case, a complete annual declaration, an eco-fee estimate or an exemption test. Add every other relevant component and reconcile the period and quantities with your records.

Questions to resolve before a declaration

  • Was packaging added by your company, supplier, marketplace or fulfilment provider? Identify each party and avoid counting the same material twice.
  • Does the quantity represent products, orders or packaging components? Convert explicitly when one order contains several units.
  • What belongs in each national filing? Confirm the responsible producer, material code, household or industrial classification and the applicable declaration method.
  • Which inputs are measured and which are estimated? Record the method and unresolved items rather than presenting assumptions as confirmed facts.

Fost Plus provides several declaration methods for its members. This preparation table does not replace MyFost or an official scheme form. See Fost Plus declaration guidance and retain a separate record for every other EU market.

Step 3: confirm the route and any mandate

For Belgium, Fost Plus’s practical guidance describes producer registration and representation for foreign producers. Confirm whether your file goes through a scheme or another applicable route, who submits it, and what must be signed. Do not use a GPSR appointment as a substitute for a PPWR EPR mandate.

Step 4: verify completion and plan reporting

Retain the submission record, confirmation, official identifier where issued, mandate and scheme documents. Confirm the reporting calendar, data owner and next update trigger. Tell a marketplace only what those records establish. An application receipt is not a guarantee of registration, an accepted listing or a particular completion date.
