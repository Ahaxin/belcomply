Packaging EPR compliance and PPWR representation

BelComply helps businesses scope packaging EPR registration, reporting and PPWR authorised representation for their EU sales countries. Start with a free initial assessment. Professional work and external contributions are quoted separately after the country, producer and packaging stream have been identified.

Request a free initial assessment

What rules frame this service?

Regulation (EU) 2025/40, the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), is the EU framework for packaging and packaging waste. The Commission identifies 12 August 2026 as its general application date. Specific provisions, transition arrangements and national procedures still require separate checking. An EU framework does not make a national registration or written mandate valid for every country.

European Commission overview and legislation · Sources reviewed: 17 November 2026

What packaging information should you prepare?

Separate product packaging from the boxes, fillers and other packaging added for delivery. Record each material and component weight, the legal selling entity, destination country and reporting period. If a warehouse or fulfilment provider packs orders, ask which materials it adds and whether it can supply item-level weights. Avoid combining all EU quantities into one total before the country split is available.

Registration, reporting or AR: which service do you need?

A registration engagement prepares the agreed application route. Reporting work uses the quantities and material data for an agreed period. Authorised representation is a separate role established through the applicable rules and a written mandate. A combined proposal can coordinate these tasks, but it should still identify each deliverable, your data responsibilities and any scheme contribution. Paying a professional fee does not itself pay every external charge.

How do countries change the packaging scope?

For Belgium, review household and commercial or industrial packaging separately and use our Belgian EPR guide to understand the distinct roles. For Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands, provide each destination and the corresponding sales route rather than assuming the Belgian process applies. Our country coverage page is a planning resource, not proof that one registration covers all markets. Tell us whether you sell directly, through marketplaces, wholesale or from local stock.

Does a packaging mandate include WEEE, batteries or GPSR?

No automatic extension should be assumed. A packaged electronic device with a battery can require separate packaging, WEEE and battery assessments. GPSR product safety and EU responsible-person support are also separately scoped services. When requesting a quote, list the physical product, its batteries and its packaging so that the proposal can distinguish the relevant waste streams and appointments.

What is free, and what is quoted?

The initial needs assessment is free and does not require you to buy a full service. Its purpose is to identify your products, sales countries and information gaps before a detailed proposal. It is not registration, ongoing reporting or a formal appointment. If you proceed, the written quote will identify deliverables, exclusions, professional fees and external charges.
