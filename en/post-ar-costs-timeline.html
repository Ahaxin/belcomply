EU authorised representative: registration timelines for 2026

5 Oct 2026 · Costs

How long does it take to appoint an EU authorised representative and register in each country? Here are the real, current timelines — so you can plan instead of guessing.

TL;DR — Registration takes 2–14 weeks depending on the member state — Belgium is fastest at 4–6 weeks, Germany takes 12–14. Fees vary by country and volume; get a quote for your setup.

What the fee actually buys

An AR fee is not a subscription for "paperwork help". It pays for a legal role:

  • Registration in the national register under the producer's name
  • Annual declarations of packaging volumes, filed on time
  • Authority contact — the AR is the named party the regulator reaches
  • Joint liability — the AR stands liable alongside you, which is why the fee exists at all

The last point is the whole reason the role is not free. In Belgium, the AR carries the same obligations as the producer — that is priced in.

Why Belgium is both fastest and worth it

Belgium registers in 4–6 weeks — up to three times faster than Germany's 12–14 weeks. When a marketplace is enforcing and your listing is at risk, speed matters more than a small saving. Belgium's AR also carries the heaviest legal weight, which is why serious providers charge a fair premium for it.

How to plan across multiple countries

If you sell into several EU countries, the fees stack. Lead times and requirements stack across countries. Two practical moves:

  1. Prioritise by volume and enforcement — Germany, France and the Netherlands are the largest markets and the most likely to enforce; start there.
  2. Bundle through one coordinator — a single partner managing all your ARs under one contract is usually cheaper and always simpler than five separate providers.

The practical takeaway: registration takes 2–14 weeks depending on the country, and the clock starts when you appoint your AR. The cost of a suspended listing far exceeds any AR fee — so start early, and start with the markets that enforce hardest.

Sources: BelComply's own timelines, verified against the market in September 2026. Always confirm current figures before planning.
