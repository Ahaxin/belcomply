EPR / PRACTICAL GUIDE

Amazon EPR Verification: Which Numbers Do You Need?

Does Amazon require one EPR number for every EU country?

There is no single EPR number that should be assumed to cover every EU country or waste stream. Check the exact marketplace notice, country, product category and issuing organisation. A packaging membership number, a battery registration and a WEEE identifier serve different purposes.

Separate packaging rollout from existing battery requirements

For the new harmonised packaging-register framework, current Commission guidance says online platforms are not yet generally required to ask for an EPR registration number through that new process. This does not remove existing national or waste-stream requirements. Bebat separately explains that marketplaces in Belgium must admit only battery producers who demonstrate participation or an approved individual plan.

How to respond to an EPR verification request

Save the notice with its date and deadline. Identify the legal selling entity and product stream. Check whether the requested field expects a public registration, scheme membership or other evidence. Compare the entity name and scope with the authentic document, then provide the requested information through the marketplace’s own process. Do not borrow another company’s number.

Check FBA packaging responsibilities separately

FBA is a fulfilment arrangement. Keep product packaging separate from shipping packaging added by a fulfilment provider, and identify which entity is responsible under the relevant national rules. A fulfilment contract alone does not establish that all EPR obligations have been transferred.

What information should you send for a review?

Provide the marketplace notice, selling-entity name, country of establishment, target country, product category and existing registration documents. We can scope EPR registration and PPWR representation across the EU, with a focus on Belgium. GPSR product-safety support is a separate service.

Amazon’s published submission fields

Amazon’s public guide asks for a 13-digit LUCID number for German packaging and relevant UINs for French EPR categories through its EPR compliance portal. German electronics follow a separate registration route.

Check the country, category, selling entity and notice in your account. Do not reuse these fields as a Belgian requirement: the public guide does not establish one universal EU submission process.
