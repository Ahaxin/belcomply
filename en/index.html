Compliance duty

Do you actually need a Belgian AR?

The test is simple: is your packaging first placed on the Belgian market, delivered directly to a Belgian end consumer, by a company without a Belgian entity? If so, you are the producer — and the law requires you to appoint a Belgian authorised representative. There is no minimum volume: the obligation applies from the very first package.

Your sales modelAR required?Why
Amazon FBA (Pan-EU / EFN), goods reaching Belgian consumers Yes Foreign company delivers directly to end users → you are the producer
Own website / eBay / bol.com shipping direct to Belgium Yes Same logic — direct distance selling to Belgian consumers
Selling to a Belgian or EU wholesaler / importer No The importer becomes the producer; the obligation shifts to them
You already have a Belgian or EU legal entity No You can register directly — no authorised representative needed

Your legal status: under Article 2, 20° of the Belgian Interregional Packaging Agreement (as amended, in force since 3 Sep 2020), a foreign company selling directly to Belgian consumers is a Type B packaging producer and must appoint an AR established in Belgium — using the word « désigne » ("must appoint"), not "may appoint".
