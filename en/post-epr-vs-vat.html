EU / EPR & VAT

EU EPR vs VAT: Separate Compliance Duties

EPR and VAT answer different questions. VAT concerns tax treatment of transactions; EPR concerns responsibility for relevant products or packaging becoming waste. A record for one regime is not evidence that the other has been completed.

What does OSS simplify?

The EU’s One Stop Shop guidance describes a route for managing VAT on qualifying cross-border consumer sales. It is a tax mechanism, not a packaging or WEEE register. Check the eligibility and transactions with the relevant tax guidance; do not read an OSS registration as an EU-wide EPR approval.

What does packaging EPR require you to review?

The Commission’s packaging guidance directs businesses to the national rules applying where their packaging becomes waste. Record the destination, responsible entity and packaging stream. A VAT identifier may appear in an application as company information, but that field does not make it the resulting EPR registration.

Primary guidance

Use two evidence folders

Keep tax registrations, VAT returns and relevant transaction records in one folder. Keep EPR registrations, scheme contracts, mandates and packaging reports in another. Use a common company-and-country index to link them, rather than changing the name of one document to satisfy a request under the other regime.

Ask for a scope by duty

For a launch into Belgium, list the seller, customer type, sales channels, packaging and any electrical equipment or batteries. Review VAT questions through the appropriate tax process and EPR questions by waste stream. BelComply’s EPR authorised representation concerns PPWR. GPSR product-safety work is separate and is not included.
