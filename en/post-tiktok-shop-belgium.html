TikTok Shop is now in Belgium — and EPR is a listing gate

16 Sep 2026 · Platforms

TikTok Shop opened to Belgian shoppers and third-party sellers on 15 June 2026. It's a fresh channel for reaching Belgian buyers — but unlike marketplaces you may already sell on, TikTok Shop Belgium verifies your EPR compliance before you can list.

Belgium joined the four-market wave

On 15 June 2026, TikTok Shop launched in Belgium, Austria, the Netherlands and Poland — its tenth European market. Belgium's audience is meaningful: roughly 4.6 million monthly active users, about 39% of the population.

Three EPR schemes, one market

Belgium runs three named EPR schemes you need to know before you sell:

  • Fost Plus (household packaging) and Valipac (industrial/commercial packaging)
  • Recupel (electrical and electronic equipment)
  • Bebat (batteries)

The Recupel gate since 29 March 2025

Here's the part most new sellers miss: since 29 March 2025, Belgian law obliges online marketplaces to verify whether sellers are Recupel members — or hold an individual waste management plan — and to refuse platform access where they are not. TikTok Shop is legally required to run this check, and it does.

Foreign sellers need an AR first

From 12 August 2026, a foreign producer must be represented by an authorised representative established in Belgium and registered with EPRiBEL. TikTok's own EPR policy tells sellers to appoint a domestic AR "where required" and upload registration numbers through Seller Center.

TikTok's onboarding is approval-gated

Belgium's default is a local entity: you must be incorporated there, hold inventory there, ship and accept returns domestically. You'll also need a business bank account at an EU institution, and a business representative holding EU identification. You cannot list products until TikTok verifies all of it.

The practical takeaway: new platform, new buyers — but compliance comes first. If you're planning TikTok Shop Belgium, line up your Fost Plus membership and Recupel registration — plus an authorised representative if you're non-EU — before you apply.

Sources: O1 — Selling on TikTok Shop Belgium and Declaer — what TikTok Shop requires.
